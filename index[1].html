ABOUT THE ARTIST

Nour Ben Fekih Ahmed

Born 28 January 2005

I am a visual artist working primarily with acrylic paint. My work moves between the human figure, movement, place, memory and atmosphere—using expressive color and contrast to turn a scene into an emotional experience.

This collection spans several years of exploration, from quiet Mediterranean light to dramatic figurative compositions. Each work is part of an evolving personal visual language.
